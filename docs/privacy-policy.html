Privacy Policy — wc3

Last updated: 2026-07-29

Overview

wc3 ("the Extension") is a browser-automation tool that acts purely as an execution endpoint. It receives operation commands from a relay service that you run yourself on your own computer (default: 127.0.0.1:3459) and carries them out in your Chrome browser using the standard chrome.* APIs (opening tabs, clicking, reading page structure, filling inputs, etc.). The Extension is designed so that your browsing data stays on your machine.

What data the Extension handles

  • Tab and window information (URLs, titles, tab/group state) — to open, focus, group, and operate on tabs.
  • Page content and accessibility structure of the active page — to locate elements and perform actions such as clicking or typing.
  • Local configuration/state stored via chrome.storage — e.g. connection settings — kept locally in your browser.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing history, page content, or personal information to us or to any third-party server.
  • We do not include analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • The Extension only communicates with the local relay you run yourself (loopback address 127.0.0.1 / localhost). It does not send your data to any remote server operated by us.

Data storage and retention

Any data the Extension uses exists only transiently in your browser to execute the current command, plus local settings in chrome.storage. Nothing is sent to or retained on servers we control. Uninstalling the Extension removes its local data.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • tabs, tabGroups — open, focus, and organize tabs to run automations.
  • scripting — inject the scripts that read page structure and perform actions.
  • storage — save local connection settings and state.
  • alarms — keep the background service worker alive / schedule reconnects.
  • declarativeNetRequest — adjust request rules where required for automation on target pages.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — automation must be able to operate on whatever page you direct it to; the Extension does not read pages you are not actively automating.

Third parties

The Extension does not share data with third parties. All command traffic is between the Extension and a relay running locally on your own device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
